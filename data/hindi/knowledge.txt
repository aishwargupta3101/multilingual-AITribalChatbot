TRIBAL COMMUNITIES
Tribal communities have diverse cultures, traditions, languages, festivals, food, clothing, arts, crafts, and traditional practices.
NORTHEAST INDIA
Northeast India has many indigenous and tribal communities with distinct languages, cultural traditions, festivals, traditional clothing, food, music, dance, and handicrafts.
TAI KHAMTI
The Tai Khamti are an indigenous community associated with Northeast India. Their culture includes distinctive traditions, language, festivals, food, clothing, architecture, music, dance, and handicrafts.
TRADITIONAL CULTURE
Traditional culture can include community festivals, traditional clothing, food preparation, music, dance, handicrafts, ceremonies, and traditional knowledge.
HANDICRAFTS
Traditional handicrafts are an important part of the cultural heritage of many tribal communities. Crafts may include weaving, bamboo and cane work, woodwork, basketry, decorative items, and traditional household objects.
TRADITIONAL FOOD
Traditional tribal food varies between communities and regions. It can include locally available vegetables, rice, herbs, fruits, meat, fish, and other locally sourced ingredients.
LANGUAGE
Tribal communities may have their own languages or varieties of languages. Language is an important part of cultural identity and traditional knowledge.
CULTURAL PRESERVATION
Preserving tribal languages, traditions, oral histories, traditional arts, handicrafts, and indigenous knowledge is important for maintaining cultural heritage.
